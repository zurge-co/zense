03 · The review habit
AI can write the code.
You review the work.

Every AI edit in Zense arrives as a reviewable diff — never a silent overwrite. You see what changed, you stage what makes sense, and you are the one who commits.

That's not a feature. It's the whole point. Zense is built around one habit: review before you commit.
